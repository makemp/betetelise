Frequently asked questions

Is my progress backed up or synced between devices?

No — everything is stored only on the device you studied on, and nothing is synced. Deleting the app deletes your progress, so keep that in mind before removing it.

I'm not getting reminders or the test-day countdown.

Check three places: the app's Settings → daily reminder toggle; iOS Settings → Notifications → PassBritain (permission must be allowed); and any active Focus mode, which can silence notifications. The test-day countdown is scheduled at 9:00 the day before your test and 8:00 on the morning itself — if you set a test date that's tomorrow after 9:00 has passed, only the morning nudge remains.

The Home Screen widget isn't updating.

Widgets refresh when you study and on a schedule controlled by iOS. If it looks stale, open the app once — that refreshes the widget. If it's still stuck, remove the widget and add it again.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Every question, mock exam, and explanation is bundled in the app. The only thing that ever uses the network is an optional check for required app updates — nothing else, and no personal data is ever sent (see our privacy policy).

Can I change my test date or daily goal?

Yes — in the app's Settings. Moving your test date automatically re-paces your study plan and reschedules the countdown notifications.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset Progress clears your answers, scores, streaks, and bookmarks. This can't be undone.

Are these the real exam questions?

No — and no app can offer those. All PassBritain questions are original works aligned to the official handbook syllabus, "Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents" (3rd edition), and the mock exams match the real test's format exactly: 24 questions, 45 minutes, 75% to pass.
