Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

PassBritain collects no personal data. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email addresses.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No crash-reporting or usage telemetry of any kind.
  • All of your progress — answers, study plan, streaks, mock results, bookmarks — is stored only on your device.

What stays on your device

Everything the app remembers is kept in a local database on your iPhone or iPad: your study plan and test date (if you choose one), your answers and scores, spaced-repetition progress, streaks, and bookmarks. This data is never transmitted, synced, or shared. Deleting the app deletes it.

Notifications

Study reminders and test-day countdowns are local notifications, scheduled entirely on your device. They require no server and involve no data leaving your phone. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

The one network call

When the app launches, it may make a single request to the developer's own server to check the minimum supported app version, so the app can tell you if an important update is required. The request contains no personal data — no identifier, no device information, nothing about you — and the response is not stored beyond that check. If the request fails (for example, you are offline), the app simply carries on.

Third parties

PassBritain uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and shares no data with anyone, because it has no data to share.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date. The principle will not change: your data stays on your device.
